# UI review · 8 October 2026

The review covers every current screen and original flow from UX, native Apple component, and accessibility perspectives. Source review includes the working tree; pixel review includes exported iOS 27 screenshots and an independent simulator framebuffer. The matrix distinguishes source review, inspected pixels and passing interaction journeys; later-source replays and external checks remain explicit. Screenshots and automated checks do not establish Apple HIG certification.

## Apple guidance

The component decisions follow Apple's guidance for [accessibility and Dynamic Type](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), [readable layouts and touch targets](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/), [data entry](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/entering-data), [text fields](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields), [native sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets), and [permission timing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy/) and [direct Settings recovery](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/settings). Native Forms, Lists, pickers, toggles, search, confirmations and sharing provide familiar behavior; app-owned copy uses explicit readable colors. Apple also explains [element-specific audit filters](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10035/?time=597) and [why automated audits do not establish complete accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/accessibility/performing-accessibility-audits-for-your-app).

## Accessibility audit

The final phone all-type diagnostic snapshot contains **30 reports requiring review: 14 contrast, 13 font-scaling and three clipping reports, with zero exemptions or decorative-raster classifications**, across library, meal, split and meal editor. The collector preserves every issue in the raw structured inventory; successful collection means the inventory was produced, not that the audit found zero issues. Seven contrast reports have unavailable element identity: library one, meal two, split two and editor two. One additional library text-clipping report has unavailable identity. These eight reports remain unresolved instrumentation gaps; none is waived.

The complete iPad all-type inventory retains **32 reports: 14 contrast, 12 font-scaling and six clipping reports**. Two contrast reports are classified as decorative raster emoji with the exact `decorative-meal-charm` identity, once in the library and once in the visible sidebar during meal review; both remain in the raw export. The other 30 require review. The six clipping reports repeat the sidebar search placeholder, compact people summary and an unidentified element across library and meal surfaces. The two unidentified clipping reports remain unresolved; none of the 32 reports is removed or waived. Named font reports cover sidebar date/total, subtotal, Items, Done, Cancel/Save and Remove receipt/Delete meal. Named contrast reports cover the primary action, section headers, native toolbar/destructive controls and an item amount/caption. Prior pixel/font evidence supports specific observed variants, not blanket clearance of these different nodes or states.

Direct rendering and AX geometry contradict some reports: the final library total height grows 18→58.7 points and meal total 40.7→71.7 points. Largest-size amount rows stack instead of overlapping. Native Cancel is black on pale glass with approximately 19.7:1 contrast despite its contrast failure. The two-line people summary has full accessible content and a detail route. These observations do not clear unrelated reports. Actual editor input height grows 22→65 points and receipt control 52→155.33; the receipt action wraps without clipping. The single-line title is visibly ellipsized when idle but retains its full AX value. Final named clipping reports identify the title input and receipt action; actual largest-size editing and receipt pixels distinguish native single-line truncation from inaccessible controls. The unidentified library clipping report cannot be classified from those named cases.

The maintained gates separately check native hit-region/description/trait/element-detection behavior, actual font growth/layout and rendered app-owned contrast. The latest affected phone strict control gate passed in 18.484 seconds without exemptions; its success does not clear the separate contrast/font/clipping inventory. The full iPad run passed the unchanged strict four-type control gate on library, meal, split and meal editor in 20.417 seconds without exemptions. The earlier centered sheets produced seven unidentified element-detection reports; their underlying cause remains unknown, and the passing focused presentation does not retroactively identify or waive those reports. The diagnostic inventory retains exact surface, audit type, element identity/label/frame where available, and all unavailable-element reports. No broad null-element or contrast exclusion is justified.
