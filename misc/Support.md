# Split My Meal support

Contact [shreyassane@outlook.com](mailto:shreyassane@outlook.com) with the app version, your iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Include a screenshot if useful; remove names, restaurant details, or receipt information you do not want to share.

Add a meal, then add the people and items from the bill. Choose who shared each item. Tax follows each person’s item share; percentage tips use the subtotal including tax. The split summary allocates remaining cents consistently so the shares and any unassigned amount equal the bill total.

An unassigned amount means some items have no selected person, or fixed charges have no priced items to divide them across. Assign the remaining items; for an empty bill, add priced items or clear the fixed charges before settling. All prices and fixed charges are in US dollars.

Restaurant search works without location access. Choose nearby search only if you want to use your location. If search is unavailable, check your connection or try a more specific name and city. A restaurant and receipt are optional.

If an old restaurant has an invalid saved location, its map shows a recovery message. Edit the meal to replace or remove the restaurant; the app keeps the existing record until you save.

To attach a receipt, edit the meal and choose a photo. Save commits the change; Cancel keeps the previous receipt. Open the saved receipt to zoom or share it. Large photos are resized for storage, so keep the original if you need its full resolution.

Meals are stored on your device and may sync through your private iCloud account. If saved meals cannot be opened, the app preserves the data and offers a retry. Do not uninstall the app while troubleshooting missing data.

Deleting a meal also deletes its items, people, restaurant, and stored receipt. Deletion requires confirmation and cannot be undone in the app.
