# Split My Meal privacy policy

Updated October 7, 2026.

Split My Meal stores the meals, people, item assignments, restaurant details, and receipt photos you enter. This data stays in the app’s storage on your device and may sync through your private iCloud account when iCloud is available. The developer does not operate a server that receives your meal data.

Restaurant search uses Apple MapKit. Search queries are sent to Apple to find places. Location is optional and requested only when you choose nearby search or Show my location on a restaurant map; searching by restaurant name works without location access. Apple’s services are governed by [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Receipt photos are selected with the system photo picker. The app receives only the photos you choose and makes a smaller copy for storage. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

Sharing a split or receipt opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination and whether to send. Shared content may include meal titles, people’s names, bill amounts, or receipt information.

The app includes no advertising, tracking SDK, analytics service, or developer-operated error collection. It does not use cookies. Support messages contain only the information you choose to send.

Deleting a meal removes its items, people, restaurant details, and stored receipt from the app. Device backups or iCloud may retain data according to your Apple account settings. Deletion is confirmed in the app and cannot be undone there.

For privacy or support questions, contact [shreyassane@outlook.com](mailto:shreyassane@outlook.com).
